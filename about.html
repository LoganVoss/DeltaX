About

Logan Voss is DeltaX.

Los Angeles musician. Fifteen years of records. Still shipping.

Work

Rap first. Then the beats took over.

The first name was LOVO. Logan was rapping, writing bars, and learning how a beat is supposed to sit under a voice. Then the instrumentals started winning. Production became the job. DeltaX became the name on the records.

He never picked one lane and stayed there. A club record one week. A meditation album the next. House, hip-hop, dance, dubstep, downtempo. If it sounded good, it went out.

25+ albums. Hundreds of releases. Fifteen years of finishing the song and putting it in the world.

Background

Three cities. One long playlist.

Born in San Francisco on Christmas Day, 1995. At ten, the family moved to Chicago. Different weather. Different radio. A house-music city that still knows hip-hop.

College brought him back west, this time to San Luis Obispo. After school he moved to Los Angeles, and that is where the catalog got built.

Reach

Give the music somewhere to go.

When the streaming graph sat still, Logan put the catalog on Pixabay, free for anyone making anything. Editors, filmmakers, and kids with timelines started cutting it in. Films, TV, ads, videos in rooms he will never walk into. Over 100,000 downloads later, the records had a life of their own.

Now

Still in the room.

Music is still the main work. Logan also shoots his own photos, designs the covers, and builds apps.

Most of the DeltaX catalog is not AI. He started using AI music tools to learn what the new stuff can actually do, not to replace how he makes records. The songs came out thin. Champagne is what he built to finish them.
