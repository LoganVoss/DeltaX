Why it exists

Built after testing the tools

Logan used AI music tools to learn the new landscape, not to replace his catalog. Most DeltaX records are still made without AI.

The tools can write a song. They do not make it sound finished. Champagne is the last step.

How it works

Four steps

01Import

WAV, AIFF, MP3, M4A, or FLAC from a generator, DAW, or voice memo.

02Pick a style

Four mastering styles. Choose the one that fits the track.

03Preview

A/B against the original. Trim and fade if you need to.

04Export

24-bit, 48 kHz WAV. Leveled and ready to release.

How

Under the hood

Champagne analyzes the track, then applies level, EQ, compression, and peak control. Processing runs on your device.

It does not use AI to master the audio. The engine is digital signal processing, built from 15 years of experience in sound design.
